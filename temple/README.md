# The Temple of the Ten

A walkable 3D cathedral for Lionel Messi, built with three.js.

**Play:** https://amirrezavahidzadeh.github.io/temple/

- A night plaza, a nave with eight chapels covering his career, two transept halls of saints (Barça, Argentina and Miami), a domed crossing, a halo of eight golden balls, and a crypt for Diego Maradona.
- Make a pilgrim, then walk, jump, sit, emote and chat.
- Multiplayer is peer-to-peer (WebRTC via [Trystero](https://github.com/dmotz/trystero)): everyone on the same server number walks together. No backend.

Controls: WASD to move, mouse to look, Space to jump, E to read/sit/light a candle, Enter to chat, 1/2/3 for emotes, V for camera, M for map.

Third-party: three.js (MIT), Trystero (MIT), Cinzel and Cormorant Garamond fonts (SIL Open Font License, via Fontsource).
