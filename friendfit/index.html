How it works

Three steps, then it becomes a habit you share.

Pick a daily goal

Choose 10 to 60 active minutes a day. Start small: a streak you keep beats a goal you skip.

Move, any way you like

Track a walk, run, ride or hike with GPS, or log gym, yoga, swimming, football or dance by hand, even after the fact.

Bring your person

Swap six-letter codes as friends or as a couple. Now you see each other’s workouts, keep a duo streak, and nudge each other.

💞 Better together

See each other move

FriendFit is built around two people, or a small crew, not a feed of strangers.

  • Where, how and how long. Every workout shows the place, the route, the time and the distance.
  • Duo streaks. Count the days you both moved. Break it and you both feel it.
  • Together bonus. Tag your partner on a shared walk and you each get ×1.5 XP.
  • Nudges. “Walk with me tonight?” “Don’t break our streak! 🔥”
  • A weekly league. You and your partners race for XP. It resets every Monday.

Install on Android

FriendFit isn’t on Google Play yet, so you install it straight from here. It takes a minute.

  1. Download FriendFit.apk on your phone (about 48 MB).
  2. Open it from your notifications or the Downloads folder.
  3. Allow this source when Android asks (your browser or Files app), then tap Install.
  4. Allow location “All the time” when FriendFit asks, so runs keep tracking with the screen off.
Download FriendFit.apk

It says “Extract”, not “Install”?

An APK is a kind of zip file, so some apps offer to unpack it. Open it with your Files app instead, or long-press it → Open with → Package installer. If its name ends in .zip, rename it to FriendFit.apk.

Play Protect warning?

That appears for any app from outside Google Play. Tap More details → Install anyway.

Want partners?

Partner features sync through a FriendFit server (free and open source, it runs on any small server). On your own, everything works offline from the first minute.
